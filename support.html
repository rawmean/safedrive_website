Frequently asked questions

How do I invite someone to see my drives?

Open the observers screen in SafeDrive, tap invite, and share the link with your family member. They'll install SafeDrive as an observer — no subscription needed on their side.

When do I get a speeding alert?

Observers are notified the moment a driver exceeds the speed limit set in the app, with optional audio alerts. You can adjust the limit and sensitivity at any time.

Does my location get shared when I'm not driving?

No. SafeDrive only shares your speed and location while a drive is active. When the drive ends, sharing stops.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to view, change or cancel.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your location is shared only with observers you invite. SafeDrive has no ads, no tracking, and never sells your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
